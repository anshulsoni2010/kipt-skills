# Meeting notes

Write, in this order:

1. **Title and date**, taken from the source if present.
2. **Summary**: up to three sentences.
3. **Decisions**: bullets, each a full sentence.
4. **Action items**: checklist with owner and due date, for example
   "- [ ] Maya: send the revised quote by Friday". Write "Owner: unclear"
   when no one was named.
5. **Open questions**: anything raised but not settled.

Leave out small talk. Keep names exactly as written in the source. Do not
add decisions or deadlines that weren't stated.
