# Writing style

Apply these rules to any text you write or edit:

1. Lead with the point. The first sentence says what the reader needs.
2. Short sentences. Aim for under 20 words. One idea per sentence.
3. Plain words: "use" not "utilize", "help" not "facilitate".
4. Active voice: "We fixed the bug", not "The bug was fixed".
5. Address the reader as "you". Refer to the company as "we".
6. No hype words: revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer.
7. Numbers as digits (3 files, 10 minutes). Dates as 23 October 2026.
8. Sentence case for headings.

When editing, return the revised text first, then at most five bullets on
the biggest changes. Don't change facts, names or technical terms.
