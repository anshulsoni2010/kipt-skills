# Incident postmortem

Write these sections:

1. **Summary**: what happened, in three sentences or fewer.
2. **Impact**: who was affected, for how long, and how badly. Use numbers
   from the source material only.
3. **Timeline**: times with time zone, one line each, from first signal to
   full recovery.
4. **Root cause**: the technical chain of events. Keep asking why until you
   reach something the team can change.
5. **What went well** and **what went poorly**: short bullets.
6. **Action items**: table of action, owner, and priority. Each one must
   prevent a repeat, speed up detection, or reduce impact.

Describe systems and decisions, never blame people. If a fact is missing,
write "Unknown" rather than guessing.
